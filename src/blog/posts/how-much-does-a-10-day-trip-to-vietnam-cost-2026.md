A mid-range 10-day trip to Vietnam costs $2,279 per person from Los Angeles, flights included. That's $277 less than the same trip to Thailand, even though the flight costs $59 more. Below is the full bill from five home cities, solo or as a couple, and whether your passport needs the $25 visa.

## **A glance at the answer**

A 10-day trip to Vietnam costs about $1,390 per person on a budget, $2,280 at mid-range and $3,630 in comfort, flying from Los Angeles. Couples who share a room pay about $1,940 each at mid-range. The flight is about 37% of the mid-range bill, and going in December adds $377 from Los Angeles. UK and German passports don't need the $25 visa that our totals include. [Skip to the calculator](https://isthisworthmymoney.com/blog/10-day-trip-to-vietnam-cost/#calculator) to price your own trip.

**How we compared:**

* One traveler, or two sharing a room. 10 days in Vietnam means 9 hotel nights.
* Route: Ho Chi Minh City (3 nights), Hoi An (3, flying into Da Nang) and Hanoi (3, with a Ha Long Bay day trip), then a flight back to Ho Chi Minh City for the flight home.
* Round trip economy flights to Ho Chi Minh City from KAYAK's 12-month data for Los Angeles, New York, Toronto, London and Frankfurt, checked 30 October to 1 November 2026. Budget uses the low end of the typical fare range, mid-range the 12-month average and comfort the high end.
* A 10% buffer on everything except flights. Rooms fit 2 people and airport cars fit 3, so those costs split when you travel together.
* Every total includes the $25 e-visa. If your passport is on one of the 45-day lists (UK, Germany and others), take £21 off a solo total from London or €25 from Frankfurt: £19 or €23 for the visa, plus its share of the buffer.
* Exchange rates from the Banque de France, 30 October 2026: $1 = CA$1.40 = £0.75 = €0.90. Dong at the Vietcombank mid rate the same day: $1 = 25,830 VND.

Plan on about $144 a day per person at mid-range once everything is counted, or $110 each as a couple. That's more than the $85 a day etravelsim quotes for mid-range, because ours adds insurance, the visa, a SIM card, ATM fees and a 10% buffer, and allows more for the room and for getting around.

The last two rows include the $25 visa for a US passport. Without it, 10 days on the ground cost $27 to $28 less. ### **Getting between cities**

* Ho Chi Minh City to Da Nang: about $45 to $72 one way by air, with VietJet fares from $25.
* Da Nang to Hanoi: 880,000 to 1,140,000 VND ($34 to $44) for a 6-berth sleeper train that takes 15 to 18 hours, a sleeper bus from 620,000 VND ($24), or about $46 to $75 one way by air.
* Hanoi back to Ho Chi Minh City: about $50 to $86 one way.
* That's $129 per person on a budget (two flights and the sleeper train), $153 at mid-range (three flights) and an estimated $233 in comfort.

## **When is the cheapest time to go to Vietnam?**

February to April is the only stretch that's dry in the north, center and south at once. December is the most expensive month to fly from four of the five cities.

| Region                     | Rainy                                            | Dry                                                  | Watch out for                                                           |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| North (Hanoi, Ha Long Bay) | May to September, peak July and August           | October to April, cool and grey December to February | Ha Long cruises sometimes cancelled in storms, May to September         |
| Center (Hoi An, Da Nang)   | September to December, peak October and November | February to August                                   | Typhoons and floods in Hoi An Ancient Town, mostly October and November |
| South (Ho Chi Minh City)   | May to November, wettest in September            | December to April                                    | Nothing beyond the rain                                                 |

* With a December flight, the mid-range trip costs $2,656 from Los Angeles ($377 more), $2,955 from New York ($503 more), CA$4,050 from Toronto (CA$384 more), £1,997 from London (£225 more) and €2,316 from Frankfurt (€153 more). These figures change the flight only; we found no reliable data on December hotel prices.
* Flying in the cheapest month brings the same trip down to $2,177 from Los Angeles (March, $102 less), $2,313 from New York (November), CA$3,483 from Toronto (September), £1,656 from London (January) and €2,084 from Frankfurt (November).
* Tet, the Lunar New Year, falls on Saturday 6 February 2027. Around Tet 2026, domestic flights, trains and buses sold out 4 to 6 weeks ahead at 2 to 3 times normal fares. Expect small shops, restaurants and museums to close around 6 to 8 February.

## **Do you need a visa for Vietnam in 2026?**

It depends on your passport. US and Canadian travelers need a $25 e-visa, while UK and German passports, along with 19 other European countries, get 45 days visa free until 2028. Our totals include the $25 for every departure city, so with one of these passports take £21 off a solo London total or €25 off Frankfurt.

| Passport                                                             | Rule, November 2026                 | Cost |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---- |
| US, Canada                                                           | e-visa, single entry, up to 90 days | $25  |
| UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland  | 45 days visa free until 14 Mar 2028 | $0   |
| Belgium, Netherlands, Poland, Switzerland, Czech Republic and 7 more | 45 days visa free until 14 Aug 2028 | $0   |
| Ireland, Austria, Portugal, Greece and other EU countries not listed | e-visa                              | $25  |

The 7 more on the second list are Bulgaria, Croatia, Hungary, Luxembourg, Romania, Slovakia and Slovenia. Ireland, Austria, Portugal and Greece appear on neither list, so check the rule for your own passport before you book. Apply for the e-visa on the official site, evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn; a multiple entry e-visa costs $50. Hotels must register foreign guests with the police within 12 hours; there is nothing for you to do or pay.

### **How much cash should you bring?**

* Foreign cards pay about 50,000 VND ($1.94) per withdrawal at Vietcombank and about 3.3% of the amount at TPBank. VPBank is known for charging no fee. Most banks cap a withdrawal at 2,000,000 to 3,000,000 VND (about $77 to $116).
* Our totals assume three withdrawals, about $6 per person.
* A Vinaphone tourist SIM (15 days, 1 GB a day) costs 199,000 VND in town plus the SIM card, or 220,000 to 250,000 VND at the airport: $8 to $9 in our totals.
* VAT is 8%, cut from 10%, until 31 December 2026. We found no general tipping rule; Ha Long guides and crew get 10,000 to 30,000 VND per person.
