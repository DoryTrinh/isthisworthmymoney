## **A glance at the answer**

Yes, China is much cheaper in dollars. Essential bills for one adult run about **$540 a month in China vs $2,300 in the US**.

But pay is lower too, so the real answer depends on who signs your paycheck. An urban Chinese worker on the official average wage keeps **59%** of take-home pay after bills. The average American keeps **50%**, or **36%** if they drive. 

**How we compared:** 

* One adult, full-time job, renting a 1-bedroom alone. 
* Take-home pay is after tax and payroll deductions. 
* Prices are national averages, converted at the ECB rate on 19 October 2026 (1 USD = 6.68 yuan). 
* Health insurance only counts when you pay it on top of your salary.

## **How much do people earn in China vs the US?**

The average American takes home about **3.5 times more** than the average urban Chinese worker. And China has two very different "average" salaries.
