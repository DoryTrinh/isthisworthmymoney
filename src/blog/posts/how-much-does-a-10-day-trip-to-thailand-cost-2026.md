## **A glance at the answer**

<p class="lead-quote">A 10-day trip to Thailand costs about $1,490 per person on a budget, $2,560 at mid-range and $4,980 in comfort, flying from Los Angeles. Couples who share a room pay about $2,190 each at mid-range. Flights are roughly a third of the bill, and going in December adds $540 to $670.</p>

> [Skip to the calculator](https://isthisworthmymoney.com/blog/10-day-trip-to-thailand-cost/#calculator) to price your own trip.

**How we compared:**

* One traveler, or two sharing a room. 10 days in Thailand means 9 hotel nights.
* Route: Bangkok, Chiang Mai and Phuket, 3 nights each, flying back to Bangkok for the flight home.
* Round trip economy flights from KAYAK's 12-month data for Los Angeles, New York, Toronto, London and Frankfurt, checked 27 October 2026.
* A 10% buffer on everything except flights. Rooms fit 2 people and airport cars fit 3, so those costs split when you travel together.
* Exchange rates from the Banque de France, 19 October 2026: $1 = ฿33.55 = CA$1.41 = £0.75 = €0.89.

## **How much does a 10-day trip to Thailand cost in total?**

About $2,560 per person at mid-range if you fly from Los Angeles, and $1,490 if you travel on a budget. Where you fly from moves the total more than most choices you make once you land.

| Flying from | Budget            | Mid-range         | Comfort           |
| ----------- | ----------------- | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| Los Angeles | $1,489            | $2,556            | $4,984            |
| New York    | $1,677            | $2,784            | $5,304            |
| Toronto     | CA$2,601 ($1,845) | CA$4,158 ($2,949) | CA$7,583 ($5,378) |
| London      | £1,147 ($1,529)   | £2,006 ($2,674)   | £3,868 ($5,158)   |
| Frankfurt   | €1,404 ($1,577)   | €2,392 ($2,688)   | €4,575 ($5,140)   |

Per person, traveling solo, flights included. Comfort travel between cities, budget and comfort airport transfers and all three insurance levels are our estimates, not quoted prices.
Here's what the mid-range $2,556 from Los Angeles pays for:

| Food and drinks               | $300   |
| Tours and tickets             | $245   |
| Travel between cities         | $180   |
| Getting around cities         | $100   |
| Airport and station transfers | $90    |

| SIM card                      | $21    |

* Flights are 31% of a mid-range trip from Los Angeles and 56% of a budget trip from Toronto. The cheaper you travel on the ground, the more the ticket decides.
* The hotel is the biggest cost after the flight, and the one a travel partner cuts in half.

### **Is it cheaper as a couple?**

Yes, by about $370 each at mid-range. Almost all of it is the hotel room you no longer pay for alone.

| Mid-range, from Los Angeles | Solo   | Couple, per person | Difference |
| --------------------------- | ------ | ------------------ | ---------- |
| Total                       | $2,556 | $2,185             | $371       |
| Hotel                       | $585   | $293               | $292       |
| Airport transfers           | $90    | $45                | $45        |

Across the three styles, a couple from Los Angeles pays about $1,340, $2,190 or $3,990 each.

**What does it cost in days of work?**

| Home    | Pay used                                           | Budget trip | Mid-range trip |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| US      | Median weekly pay, $1,239 (BLS, Q3 2026)           | 6 days      | 10.3 days      |
| UK      | Median weekly pay, £811 (ONS, April 2026)          | 7.1 days    | 12.4 days      |
| Germany | Average monthly pay, €4,784 (Destatis, April 2025) | 6.4 days    | 10.8 days      |
| Canada  | Average weekly pay, CA$1,347 (StatCan, July 2026)  | 9.7 days    | 15.4 days      |

The US and UK figures use median pay. Germany and Canada only publish a recent average, which runs higher than the median, so a typical worker there needs a few more days than shown. All figures are before tax.
The trip costs time as well as money. Twelve days away, counting the flights, take about 8 workdays, out of the 11 paid vacation days a typical US worker gets after a year.
**How much are flights to Thailand from the US, Canada, the UK and Germany?**

Expect about $780 to $1,180 for a round trip in economy, depending on your city. Los Angeles is the cheapest of the five and Toronto the most expensive.

| From        | Typical range    | 12-month average | Cheapest month   | December |
| ----------- | ---------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| Los Angeles | $670 to $957     | $784             | March, $684      | $1,131   |
| New York    | $858 to $1,277   | $1,012           | April, $879      | $1,499   |
| Toronto     | $1,026 to $1,351 | $1,177           | February, $1,086 | $1,467   |
| London      | $710 to $1,131   | $902             | May, $730        | $1,255   |
| Frankfurt   | €675 to €991     | €815 ($916)      | May, €689        | €1,022   |

Round trip, economy, per person, to Bangkok. KAYAK reports Toronto and London fares in US dollars; Toronto's average is about CA$1,660.

* December costs 25% more than the yearly average from Toronto and Frankfurt, and 48% more from New York.
* New York pays about $230 more than Los Angeles on an average ticket.
* The cheapest months fall in the hot season: March from Los Angeles, April from New York, May from London and Frankfurt. Toronto is the exception, with February cheapest.

## **How much money do you need per day in Thailand?**

Plan on about $175 a day per person at mid-range once everything is counted, or $140 each as a couple. That's more than the $60 to $120 most guides quote, because those numbers usually cover a room, meals and local rides, and nothing else.

| Per day                                           | Budget | Mid-range | Comfort |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------ | --------- | ------- |
| Room, per night                                   | $25    | $65       | $180    |
| Food, per person                                  | $12    | $30       | $75     |
| Getting around the city, per person               | $4     | $10       | $22     |
| Everything except flights, solo                   | $82    | $177      | $403    |
| Everything except flights, per person in a couple | $67    | $140      | $304    |

The last two rows add travel between cities, tours, insurance, a SIM card, ATM fees and the 10% buffer, spread over 10 days. For scale, a meal at a cheap local restaurant costs ฿100 (about $3) in Bangkok, ฿75 in Chiang Mai and ฿150 in Phuket, according to Numbeo.

**Tours and tickets**

| Item                                      | Price                                   |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Grand Palace                              | ฿500 ($15)                              |
| Wat Pho / Wat Arun / Doi Suthep           | ฿300 / ฿200 / ฿30                       |
| Elephant Nature Park, half day / full day | ฿2,500 / ฿3,500 ($75 / $104)            |
| Cooking class in Chiang Mai               | ฿1,200 to ฿1,800 ($36 to $54)           |
| Phi Phi speedboat day trip                | $42 to $110, plus a ฿400 ($12) park fee |

Our totals use $95 per person on a budget (temples, a basic island trip, one massage), $245 at mid-range (adding a cooking class and a half day at the elephant park) and $390 in comfort.

### **Getting between cities**

* Bangkok to Chiang Mai: ฿688 to ฿1,038 ($21 to $31) for a second class sleeper train, or about $45 one way by air.
* Chiang Mai to Phuket: from $90 one way. Phuket back to Bangkok: about $41.
* That's $160 per person on a budget (train plus two flights), $180 at mid-range (three flights) and an estimated $340 in comfort (full service airlines with checked bags).

### **Is $1,000 enough for 10 days in Thailand?**

On the ground, yes, if you travel on a budget: 10 days cost about $820 solo or $670 each as a couple. With flights from North America or Europe, no. The cheapest total in our numbers is $1,344 per person, for a budget couple flying from Los Angeles.

## **When is the cheapest time to go to Thailand?**

March to May usually brings the cheapest flights from these cities, and December is the most expensive month from all five. Hotels follow the same pattern, peaking from mid December to February.

| Season             | Months               | What it does to the price                                                               |
| ------------------ | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cool season (peak) | November to February | Phuket hotels 30% to 50% higher; December flights 25% to 48% above average              |
| Hot season         | March to May         | Cheapest flights from Los Angeles (March), New York (April), London and Frankfurt (May) |
| Rainy season       | May to October       | Chiang Mai rooms about $34 a night, against $63 at peak                                 |

* The same mid-range trip from Los Angeles costs $3,100 to $3,230 in December, against $2,556 on average. That's $540 to $670 more for the same 10 days.
* Rainy season prices come with closures. Maya Bay, the Phi Phi beach most island tours stop at, was closed from 1 August to 30 September 2026, so check before you book a tour for those months.

## **Do you need a visa for Thailand in 2026?**

Not for a 10-day trip if you hold a US, Canadian, UK or EU passport. Since 15 September 2026 you get 30 days visa free, down from 60.

| Item                                | Status, October 2026                                                           | Cost     |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- |
| Visa exemption (US, Canada, UK, EU) | 30 days since 15 September 2026, with one 30-day extension possible            | $0       |
| TDAC digital arrival card           | Required, filled in online before you land                                     | Free     |
| Tourist entry fee                   | Proposed at 450 baht, not collected yet; collection is targeted for early 2027 | $0 today |

Some popular budget guides still say a 300 baht entry fee is charged on arrival. It isn't. If it starts in 2027, expect about $13 more per person. Planning a much longer stay? Our guide to [retiring in Thailand on Social Security](https://isthisworthmymoney.com/blog/retire-in-thailand-on-social-security/) covers the long-stay visas.

### **How much cash should you bring?**

* Thai ATMs charge foreign cards ฿220 to ฿250 per withdrawal. AEON machines charge ฿150, with a ฿20,000 limit per withdrawal.
* Our totals assume three withdrawals, about $20 per person. Taking out larger amounts less often keeps that number down.
* VAT is 7%, and many hotels and restaurants add a 10% service charge on top. Where there's no service charge, a 10% tip or rounding up the bill is normal in Bangkok.
